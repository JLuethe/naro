The candidate's safe-save policy is same-directory exclusive temporary creation, checked complete write/metadata/file sync/close, rename commit, and parent-directory sync. Before rename, ordinary tested failures preserved pathname, bytes, inode, and required metadata. After rename, directory-sync failure reports uncertain durability and leaves the buffer modified. Final symlinks are refused; hard links split; successful regular saves replace the inode. `INSECURE_BACKUP` remains a documented compatibility option and did not reopen an alias for truncation. Backup publication uses an exclusive sibling temporary and identity-checked cleanup; lock cleanup retains and compares the opened lock inode. These policies intentionally differ from nano's in-place behavior. The source implementation is at [native/bridge.c:296](../../native/bridge.c), [src/files.rs:277](../../src/files.rs), [src/files.rs:357](../../src/files.rs), and [src/files.rs:600](../../src/files.rs).
